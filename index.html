From our family to yours.

Luminescent is a family-run coffee bar and cocktail lounge on East Colonial Drive, just past Alafaya Trail. Co-owners Akshar and Shivam built it around their Indian heritage and one idea: the same room should feel right at 9 AM and at 11 PM.

The bar

A U-shaped bar in deep green marble anchors the room, wrapped in warm wood. Sit up front, sink into a couch, or tuck into the seats in back. The espresso machines are built under the counter, so your barista works facing you.

The flavors

India is the spice capital of the world, and the menu leans in. Cardamom and rose water in a latte. Pistachio and soan papdi on espresso. Tamarind and vadouvan behind the bar at night. The classics are always there too.

The family

The signature coffees are named for the owners' mothers, and the house cocktails honor the dads. Walk in as a stranger and you will be treated like family.

The light

The name is the point. Bright, easy light for working and catching up by day. Low, glowing lamps for the lounge once the sun goes down.
